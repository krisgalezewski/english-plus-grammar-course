6 Sentence transformation

Rewrite each sentence so it means the same thing, starting with the word given.

Original: "By the time the guests arrive, cooking will have been finished."

Rewrite starting with "We": We cooking by the time the guests arrive.

Original: "It's not necessary for you to attend the meeting."

Rewrite starting with "You": You attend the meeting.

Original: "I'm almost certain that's her car in the driveway."

Rewrite starting with "That": That be her car in the driveway.

Original: "It's obvious now that she worked incredibly hard for months before the launch."

Rewrite starting with "She": She incredibly hard for months before the launch.
