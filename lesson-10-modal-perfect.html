Modal Perfect: Should Have, Could Have, Would Have

Judging the past, instead of just describing it.

1 Warm-up — from guessing to judging

Lesson 9 covered guessing about the past — must have, can't have. Today covers something related but different: judging the past, based on what should or shouldn't have happened, or what was genuinely possible but didn't.

1. Today's forms are mostly for expressing…

2. "You should have called me" mainly expresses…

3. "She could have won the race" (but she stopped to help someone) describes…

💡 Same grammar shape as Lesson 9 (modal + have + past participle) — but this time, it's judgement, not a guess.

3 The concept

Same shape, new job

Every form here uses the exact same structure as Lesson 9 — modal + have + past participle — but instead of guessing what happened, you're judging it: was it the right choice, the wrong choice, or a chance that slipped by? Browse each below, or test yourself with Quick check.

View
Judgement

— Click the pills to see another example.

✅ Regret / criticism of inaction: should have + past participle — e.g. "I should have called you sooner."
✅ Criticism of an action taken: shouldn't have + past participle — e.g. "He shouldn't have said that in the meeting."
✅ Missed opportunity: could have + past participle — e.g. "She could have taken the earlier train."
✅ Hypothetical past outcome: would / wouldn't have + past participle — e.g. "I would have called, but I didn't have your number." / "I wouldn't have called so late, but it was an emergency."
🎯 The tricky part: "should have" and "shouldn't have" aren't a simple opposite pair pointing at the same event — they criticise two different things.

✅ "You should have locked the door." — you didn't lock it, and that was the mistake.
✅ "You shouldn't have left the door unlocked." — same situation, but now the criticised action is leaving it unlocked, not a missing action.

In practice, pick whichever one matches what actually happened: something that wasn't done (should have) or something that was (shouldn't have).
