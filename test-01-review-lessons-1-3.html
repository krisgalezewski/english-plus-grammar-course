6 Sentence transformation

Rewrite each sentence so it means the same thing, starting with the word given.

Original: "It's my first time visiting Tokyo."

Rewrite starting with "I" and using "never": I Tokyo before.

Original: "The report was finished by Maria yesterday."

Rewrite starting with "Maria": Maria the report yesterday.

Original: "The phone rang while I was in the middle of cooking."

Rewrite starting with "I": I when the phone rang.

Original: "This is the third time this year the company has opened a new office."

Rewrite starting with "The company": The company three new offices this year.
