6 Sentence transformation

Rewrite each sentence so it means the same thing, starting with the word given.

1. If I enough money, I would buy a new car.

Original: "She didn't apologise because she isn't very brave."

2. If she more brave, she would have apologised.

Original: "I don't know how to dance, and I regret it."

3. I wish I how to dance.

Original: "'Close the door,' she said to him."

4. She told him the door.

Original: "He continued to work despite being tired."

5. He kept despite being tired.

Original: "Marek solved the problem, not Anna."
