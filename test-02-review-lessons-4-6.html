6 Sentence transformation

Rewrite each sentence so it means the same thing, starting with the word given.

Original: "This is the first time I've read this report."

Rewrite starting with "I" and using "never": I this report before.

Original: "By the time the deadline arrived, the team had already finished the project."

Rewrite starting with "The team": The team the project before the deadline arrived.

Original: "I've made a decision — I'm retraining as a data analyst next year."

Rewrite starting with "I": I retrain as a data analyst next year.

Original: "It's confirmed: the client and I have a call scheduled for 3pm tomorrow."

Rewrite starting with "I": I the client at 3pm tomorrow.
